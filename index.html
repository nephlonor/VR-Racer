VR-Racer

Drive a virtual car around your real room. Crash into your real walls.

Before you start (iPhone 15 Pro)

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Advanced → Feature Flags.
  2. Turn on WebXR Device API.
  3. Return here and tap Start AR.

LiDAR is used implicitly by ARKit to detect walls and the floor — accuracy is best on iPhone Pro models.

Pan your phone around the room to scan walls and floor…
